Editorial / Comparison

Co-op puzzle games on iPhone for players who like PICO PARK.

PICO PARK has no native iPhone or iPad release. So the useful question is not how to force a PC game onto a phone — it is which native iOS games actually deliver the same thing: real-time teamwork, 2D platforming, keys, doors, and puzzles that only open when everyone moves together.

By Alulim Games Verified: 2026-09-22
Language: English Español Français Deutsch Português (BR)

Disclosure: Alulim Games publishes Pippin Plaza, one of the nine games compared here. We apply the same criteria to every title, link to each game's own App Store listing, and describe our own game's monetisation the same way we describe everyone else's.

Is PICO PARK available on iPhone?

No. As of 2026-09-22, the official platform listings for PICO PARK, PICO PARK 2 and the announced SONIC PICO PARK do not include a native iPhone or iPad release. PICO PARK lists Nintendo Switch / Steam. PICO PARK 2 lists Steam, Switch, Xbox, Windows with 2–8 players. SONIC PICO PARK is dated November 12, 2026 for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Windows, Steam.

There is an official App Store app called TecoGamePad, and it is linked from PICO PARK's own site — but it is a companion controller. It turns a phone into a gamepad for a supported computer version on the same local network. It does not contain the game.

A third-party app-analytics site (Apptopia) carries an entry for App Store ID 1583471620 under the title “Pico Park”. We could not verify who published that entry, and we are not claiming no such app ever existed. What we can verify is this: Apple's current lookup for that ID returns no listing in the U.S. and Japanese storefronts, and TECOPARK's official pages list no native iPhone or iPad release for any PICO PARK title. An App Store search for “pico park” on 2026-09-22 returned no PICO PARK title; the top results were co-op puzzle games from other developers.

If you want this kind of game on an iPhone today, you need a game built for iOS. Nine candidates are compared below.

How we compared them

Every entry was checked against its current App Store listing on 2026-09-22. Where a listing is ambiguous or contradicts itself, we say so rather than resolving it in the game's favour.

  • Native iOS: it must run on iPhone and iPad directly, not stream or remote-play from a computer.
  • Real online co-op: separate people on separate devices, in real time — not just two thumbs on one screen.
  • Player count: exactly two, or genuinely more than two.
  • Solo route: whether one person can make progress without a partner.
  • Business model: price, ads and in-app purchases, described identically for every game including our own.

Game by game

01

Pippin Plaza: Online Co-op

Seller on the App Store: Atmane El Bouazzaoui (Alulim Games) · App Store

Pippin Plaza is a native iOS co-op puzzle platformer built around a private room code: you create a room, share the code, and 2 to 4 people play together in real time on their own devices. The campaign is 53 numbered handmade quests plus a separate tutorial, built on keys, doors, moving platforms and timing that only works if the team coordinates. Alone, you control two Pippins and switch between them when a puzzle needs both.

The only game in this comparison that combines native iOS, 2D side-on puzzle-platforming, real online play for more than two people, and a solo route. It is also ours, which is why its monetisation is described in the same words as everyone else's: free, ads, optional purchases.

02

Fire and Water Online Co-op

Seller on the App Store: ALSA GAMES · App Store

A mobile take on the Fireboy-and-Watergirl formula, and the most complete free option for a pair. One character survives fire, the other water; you combine them across 200+ handcrafted temple levels. The listing describes four modes: online co-op with friends or matchmaking, a 1v1 duel mode, a solo mode where you control both heroes, and local co-op on one device.

The strongest free pick if there are exactly two of you. It will not scale to three or four.

03

BOKURA

Seller on the App Store: Kodansha Ltd. · App Store

A paid, two-player-only puzzle adventure where each player sees a different world on their own screen — the same character may appear to one of you as an animal and to the other as a robot. Two people, two devices, no exceptions. Kodansha's listing is explicit that the game includes no voice chat, so remote pairs need Discord or a phone call alongside it.

The most interesting design here, and the least flexible. Two people who enjoy talking through a problem will get the most out of it.

04

Pepelo - Adventure CO-OP Game

Seller on the App Store: Tafusoft · App Store

Pepelo runs real-time online co-op for exactly two players through a room code, across 50 levels of buttons, moving platforms and timing. It is genuinely cooperative, but the view is 3D isometric rather than 2D side-on, which changes how you read a jump. An offline mode lets one player control both characters. The listing notes the first 10 levels are free and the rest unlock through a purchase.

A good 3D alternative for two. Not the one to pick if the 2D side-on view is the part you actually want.

05

Online Cats – Multiplayer Park

Seller on the App Store: FunPotion Inc. · App Store

A free key-and-door puzzle game, and structurally the closest thing here to PICO PARK's shape. It is worth being careful with the claims: the App Store description says it “can be played offline and online for 2-8 players” in one line and advertises “Multiplayer for up to 10 Online Players” a few lines later. We could not verify working live multiplayer with other people, and its App Store rating is among the lowest in this group.

Marked unverified rather than recommended or dismissed. The listing does not settle its own player count.

06

Animal Park 2 – Puzzle Games

Seller on the App Store: FunPotion Inc. · App Store

From the same developer as Online Cats, and built on the same keys-and-doors idea. Its listing opens by saying the game “can be played offline” and then uses online and multiplayer language further down, without describing a room code, invite or matchmaking flow. We found no clear evidence of live online co-op.

Treat it as a single-player puzzle game with multiplayer-sounding copy until the listing says otherwise.

07

Level Devil - NOT A Troll Game

Seller on the App Store: WebVentures B.V. · App Store

A precision trap platformer with 240+ levels, built on the joke that the level is actively trying to kill you: floors vanish, ceilings drop, spikes appear where you just looked. It is very good at that. What it is not is an online co-op game — the listing offers a local two-player mode on one device and nothing more.

Included because people looking for co-op platformers on iPhone keep finding it. Great solo; not a remote-teamwork game.

08

Crossy Road Castle

Seller on the App Store: Hipster Whale Pty Ltd · App Store

The other genuinely online multiplayer entry here, for one to four players, either on one device with controllers or across several devices. It is an endless, procedurally generated tower climb rather than a set of hand-built puzzle rooms — closer to arcade chaos than to a key-and-door puzzle. It requires an Apple Arcade subscription.

Excellent four-player co-op and no ads at all, thanks to Arcade. A different genre from PICO PARK, though.

09

Crunchyroll: Pikuniku

Seller on the App Store: Crunchyroll, LLC · App Store

A well-liked physics puzzle-adventure with a distinctive art style, now distributed through Crunchyroll's Game Vault. Its cooperative content is a set of custom local multiplayer levels, not online play. The download is free but the game requires a Crunchyroll Mega or Ultimate membership, and it needs iOS 18 or later.

A strong single-player game with local co-op attached. Not an answer to the online question.

About Pippin Plaza, in full

Pippin Plaza is our game, so here is the detail rather than the sales pitch.

  • 53 numbered handmade quests, plus a separate tutorial. The tutorial sits outside the numbered run and does not count toward the 53.
  • Online co-op for 2 to 4 players. You create a private room and share the code; everyone plays on their own device, in real time.
  • Solo play by controlling two Pippins. Alone, you work through the quest campaign by switching between two characters when a puzzle needs both — you are not driving them simultaneously.
  • Rush, Survival and Marathon are separate modes beyond the quest campaign.
  • Free to download, with ads and optional in-app purchases. No subscriptions. The optional No Forced Ads purchase removes automatic forced ads; voluntary rewarded ads remain.

Questions people actually ask

Is there a PICO PARK mobile version for iPhone?

No. The official listings for PICO PARK, PICO PARK 2 and SONIC PICO PARK cover Steam, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, Xbox, PS5 and Windows — not iOS. Any site offering a “PICO PARK mobile APK” or an iOS download is not distributing an official release.

What is TecoGamePad, then?

It is a free companion app that turns your iPhone or iPad into a gamepad for a supported computer version of PICO PARK, over the same local network. You still need the computer game.

Which iPhone games support 4-player online co-op?

Of the nine we checked, two: Pippin Plaza (2–4, private room codes, puzzle platformer) and Crossy Road Castle (1–4, online party, endless arcade climber, requires Apple Arcade).

Can I play any of these alone?

Yes. Pippin Plaza, Fire and Water Online Co-op and Pepelo all let one person control both characters. BOKURA is the clear exception — it cannot be played alone.

Why do searches for “pico park” on the App Store show other games?

Because several developers target that phrase. On 2026-09-22 an App Store search for “pico park” returned no PICO PARK title; the first two results were Online Cats and Animal Park 2. We could not verify working live online multiplayer in either, so both are marked unverified above.

Are two different apps called “Fire and Water”?

Yes, and it is easy to pick the wrong one. The game in this comparison is <em>Fire and Water Online Co-op</em> by ALSA GAMES, App Store ID 1508581240. A separate app with a near-identical name exists from a different developer.
