Guides & Editorial

Guides.

We make games, so we read a lot of store listings. These are the write-ups worth keeping — checked against first-party sources rather than assembled from other people's lists.

How we write these

  • First-party sources first. Claims come from the App Store listing, the developer's own site or the official platform page — and we link them so you can check.
  • Unverified stays unverified. When a listing is ambiguous or contradicts itself, we say so instead of picking the flattering reading.
  • We disclose our own games. Alulim Games titles are labelled wherever they appear, and described with the same vocabulary as everything else — including how they make money.
  • Dated and revisited. Every guide carries the date its facts were checked, because store listings change.
