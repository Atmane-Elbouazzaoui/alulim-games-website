iPhone games four people can actually play together online

Search for this and you mostly get games where four people crowd around one phone, games that are really two-player, and games that are only co-op on PC. This list is the ones where four separate people, on four separate devices, in four separate places, can genuinely play together.

By Alulim Games Verified: 2026-09-23

The short answer

Five games state on their own App Store listing that four people can play together online on iPhone or iPad: Bloons TD 6, Human: Fall Flat, Minecraft, Pippin Plaza and Terraria. Terraria supports up to eight; the other four top out at four.

Four of the five are paid apps. Pippin Plaza is the free one, and it shows ads. Minecraft's online mode needs a free Xbox Live account.

The most common mistake in lists on this topic is Stardew Valley, which has co-op on desktop and console but not on iPhone. Apple's own listing says so: “multiplayer functionality not supported”.

Disclosure: Alulim Games publishes Pippin Plaza, one of the five verified entries below. It is listed alphabetically alongside the others, judged against the same criteria, and its monetisation is described in the same words we use for everything else. Where another game does something better, this page says so.

What had to be true to make the list

The criteria were fixed before the games were chosen. A game is in the verified table only if a first-party source states all four of these.

  • It runs natively on iPhone or iPad. Not streamed, not remote-played from a computer, not console-only.
  • The multiplayer is with other people. Not bots, not asynchronous turns, not leaderboards.
  • The multiplayer is online. Four people in four different places, not four people around one screen.
  • Four players fit in one session. A game capped at two is a good game and the wrong answer to this question.

Every classification below quotes the sentence it rests on. Where a listing establishes one half of the test but not the other, the game goes in the second section with the gap named, rather than being quietly promoted or quietly dropped.

Questions people actually ask

Which iPhone games support four players online?

Of everything we checked, five state it on their own App Store listing: Bloons TD 6, Human: Fall Flat, Minecraft, Pippin Plaza and Terraria. Terraria goes to eight; the other four top out at four.

Is Stardew Valley co-op on iPhone?

No. Apple's listing for the iOS version ends with “multiplayer functionality not supported”. The PC and console versions do have co-op, which is why it keeps appearing in mobile co-op lists by mistake.

What is the difference between local and online multiplayer here?

Local multiplayer means everyone shares one device or one network. Online multiplayer means four people in four different places can play together. Many of the top results for this search are local-only apps, including “1 2 3 4 Player Games”, whose own listing says “all together on the same device”.

Are there free options?

Pippin Plaza is free to download, with ads and optional in-app purchases. The other four verified entries are paid apps: Bloons TD 6 is US$6.99, Minecraft is US$6.99, Human: Fall Flat and Terraria are US$4.99 each. Prices are U.S. storefront figures checked on 2026-09-23.

Do any of them need an account or subscription?

Minecraft's four-player online mode requires a free Xbox Live account, and its Realms Plus private server is a separate paid subscription. Crossy Road Castle requires an Apple Arcade subscription. The others need no account beyond the App Store.

Can any of these be played alone?

Yes, all five verified entries have a single-player route. Bloons TD 6 states its single player works offline, and Pippin Plaza's campaign can be played solo by controlling two characters.
