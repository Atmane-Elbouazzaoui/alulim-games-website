Frequently asked
I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device only, not to an account or the cloud. Deleting the app, or moving to a new device, does not carry it across. If your save is present but the game will not load it, the game keeps a backup copy and will try that automatically — email us if it does not recover.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded advertisements are always optional and are never required to progress. The game is designed and balanced to be completable without watching a single one.

How do I change my privacy or ad settings?

In the game, open Settings → Privacy choices. You can review or withdraw your advertising consent there at any time. On iOS you can also change tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. There are none.

Is the game suitable for children?

Our games are not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

I found a bug.

Please tell us. Include what you were doing when it happened, your device model and OS version, and a screenshot if you have one.
