Frequently asked
I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device only, not to an account or the cloud. Deleting the app, or moving to a new device, does not carry it across. If your save is present but the game will not load it, the game keeps a backup copy and will try that automatically — email us if it does not recover.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Where a game shows advertising, rewarded advertisements are always optional and are never required to progress. Our games on the Amazon Appstore are ad-free.

How do I change my privacy or ad settings?

In a game that shows advertising, open Settings → Privacy choices. You can review or withdraw your advertising consent there at any time. You can also change tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Our games on the Amazon Appstore do not show ads.

Are there in-app purchases?

It depends where you got the game. Our App Store games currently have none. Our Amazon Appstore games may offer optional one-off unlocks. Some add content or options — in Bills Due that is two further campaign workshops, a reward redraw on every bill, and harder optional rules for Overtime. The rest are cosmetic: piggy-bank finishes, alternative hammers, a redressed workshop, and alternative sound sets. They are permanent, not subscriptions, and a game that offers them is complete without any of them. Cosmetic items change nothing about how a game plays.

Purchases are handled by the store, not by us. If one does not appear, open the shop and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by the store under its own policy; a refunded unlock is removed the next time the game checks with that store.

If a purchase is requested from a child profile, the store sends it to a parent for approval and the unlock arrives once approved.

Is the game suitable for children?

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Our titles state an audience of 13 and over. Our games on the Amazon Appstore carry no advertising.

I found a bug.

Please tell us. Include what you were doing when it happened, your device model and OS version, and a screenshot if you have one.

Press enquiries. Use the same address and put “Press” in the subject line.
