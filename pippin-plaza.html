A little coordination goes a long way.

Jump between platforms, work switches, move crates and collect the key that opens the exit. In online co-op, each player controls a Pippin, and getting the whole party through takes coordination. A poorly timed button can make everyone rethink the plan.

A puzzle partner, even when you play alone.

Solo quest play puts two Pippins in your hands. Switch between them to set up the next move, solve the puzzle and guide both characters to the exit.

More ways to take on the quests.

Beyond the quest campaign, Rush, Survival and Marathon offer timed challenges, a daily quest gauntlet and a full run through the campaign. Modifiers introduce extra twists along the way.

Playing and purchases

Pippin Plaza is free to download and includes advertising and optional in-app purchases. Purchases include Diamond packs, a Starter Bundle and No Forced Ads. No Forced Ads removes automatic forced ads; optional rewarded ads remain available. There are no subscriptions.
