Smash. Stack. Pay up.

You have a desk, a hammer and a bill with a deadline. Every run lasts a few frantic seconds: aim, crack goofy blocky eggs with names like Cashino Soldino and Dinosaurino Spinellino, and stack your bag. Then decide: pay down the bill, or buy upgrades that make the next run hit harder. Just don't spend the bill money. That's an L.

The desk fights back

Bills don't wait quietly in a menu. When one attacks, a spot on your desk gets yeeted and there is less room to earn. Time Eggs buy you extra seconds; boss eggs and desk props shake up the run. Miss a deadline and you're cooked: you go bankrupt and the workshop starts again, but your Aura stays with you for permanent upgrades like Sigma Grip.

What is inside

  • Short, frantic runs, in Classic or in Manual mode with an aim pad and a SMASH button.
  • A Bonus Drop after every paid bill: pick one, the other one is gone fr.
  • An egg collection with character cards and mastery stamps, plus Daily W goals.
  • Brainrot voice lines, meme sounds, and W runs you can share with friends.
  • A campaign of workshops that leads into endless Overtime.

Formerly Bills Due

Brainrot Bills is the new name of Bills Due: Piggy Bank Smash. It is the same app, so updating keeps your progress and your purchases.

Out now

Brainrot Bills: Smash an Egg is free to download for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. It is supported by ads shown between runs: watching one is never required to play or to progress, there are no rewarded ads, and any purchase removes the ads between runs. Optional one-off unlocks: Remove Ads, Reroll Every Bill, Overtime Rules, Workshops 7 and 8, Hammers, and the looks-and-sound-only Guest Eggs, Crib Glow-Up and Vibe Pack.
