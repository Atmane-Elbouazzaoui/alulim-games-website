Bills Due: Smash to Pay

Smash piggy banks on your desk to pay escalating bills before the deadline.

What it is

You have a workbench, a hammer, and a stack of bills that will not wait. Break piggy banks, coins and tokens for cash, then decide what to do with it: pay down what you owe, or buy the upgrades that make the next run earn more.

The twist

An unpaid bill does not sit quietly in a menu. As its deadline approaches it encroaches on your desk, taking up the space your targets would have spawned in — so the longer you delay, the less each run earns, and the harder paying becomes. Settling it early clears your desk but spends the money you wanted for upgrades. That trade is the game.

Also in there

  • Runs bounded by stamina, not by a timer.
  • A jackpot that arms on consecutive direct hits and pays out on the next break.
  • Bankruptcy that is survivable: your cash converts to a permanent legacy currency and the cycle restarts stronger.
  • A collection cabinet recording every kind of target you have broken.

Platforms

iOS and Android. Free to play, supported by advertising. No in-app purchases. Watching ads is always optional and never required to progress.
