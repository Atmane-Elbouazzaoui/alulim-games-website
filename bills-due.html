Break. Bank. Decide.

You have a workbench, a hammer, and a stack of bills that will not wait. Break piggy banks, coins, and tokens for cash, then make the choice that drives every cycle: pay down what you owe, or buy the upgrades that make the next run earn more.

The desk fights back

An unpaid bill does not sit quietly in a menu. As its deadline approaches it encroaches on your desk, taking up the space where targets would have spawned. The longer you delay, the less each run earns—and the harder paying becomes.

Settling early clears your desk but spends the money you wanted for upgrades. Holding your nerve preserves your cash, at a cost you can see. That trade is the game.

What is inside

  • Runs bounded by stamina, not by a timer.
  • A jackpot that arms on consecutive direct hits and pays out on the next break.
  • Survivable bankruptcy: your cash converts to permanent Salvage and the cycle restarts stronger.
  • A collection cabinet that records every kind of target you have broken.

Built for mobile

Bills Due: Smash to Pay is coming to iOS and Android. It is free to play and supported by advertising, with no in-app purchases. Watching rewarded ads is optional and never required to progress.
