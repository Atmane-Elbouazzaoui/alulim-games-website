The name

An old name for new play.

Alulim is the first name in the Sumerian King List—the earliest ruler anyone bothered to write down, on a clay tablet, in a ledger of kings.

We liked that recorded history begins with a list. We liked it even more because our first original title is about a ledger you cannot escape.

Alulim Games is a small independent developer and publisher building in Unity for mobile platforms.
