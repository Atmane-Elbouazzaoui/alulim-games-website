Our games

Bills Due: Smash to Pay — in development. Smash piggy banks on a workbench to pay bills that will not wait. Every unpaid bill takes up space on your desk, so the longer you leave it, the less each run earns. Read more.

Pippin Plaza — released. A co-op puzzle game for mobile, with 53 quests built around teamwork and chaos. It keeps its own site at pippinplaza.com.

The studio

Alulim is the first name in the Sumerian King List — the earliest ruler anyone bothered to write down, on a clay tablet, in a ledger of kings. We liked that a list of names is where recorded history starts, and we liked it more because our first game is about a ledger you cannot escape.

We are a small independent studio. We build in Unity, we ship on iOS and Android, and we care most about the moment a system stops being confusing and starts being yours.

Questions, press, or bug reports: support@alulimgames.com.
